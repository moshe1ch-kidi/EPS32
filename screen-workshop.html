Getting started

        
    
          
  1. Plug the board into the computer with a USB-C cable.
    2. 
          
  2. First time only: click First-time setup and install MicroPython.
    3. 
          
  3. Click Connect board and pick the board from the list.
    4. 
          
  4. Drag blocks and click Run. The result shows up on the screen.
    5. 
          
  5. Save to board keeps your program, so it runs every time the board turns on.
    6. 
        

        
The screen

        
0,0171,319

          
Point x=0, y=0 is the top-left corner. x grows to the right up to 171, and y grows downward up to 319.

        
Tips

        
    
          
  • The screen shows English letters, numbers and symbols.
    • 
          
  • The BOOT button on the board also works as a button for your programs.
    • 
          
  • Tilt is measured in degrees, from about -90 to 90.
    • 
          
  • If the board stops responding: click Stop. If that doesn't help, press RESET on the board and connect again.
    •
